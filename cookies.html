Cookie Policy

How Webflora uses cookies and similar technologies.

1. Overview

Webflora ("Webflora", "we", "us", or "our") uses cookies and similar technologies on this website to improve website functionality, understand how visitors use our website, and provide a better user experience.

2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember information about your visit and can support website functionality, analytics, and marketing activities.

3. How We Use Cookies

Webflora may use cookies for purposes including:

  • Keeping the website functioning properly.
  • Remembering preferences and settings.
  • Understanding website traffic and visitor behavior.
  • Measuring the effectiveness of our marketing campaigns.
  • Improving our website, content, and services.

4. Analytics Cookies

We may use analytics technologies such as Google Analytics or similar services to understand website traffic, page views, interactions, and other usage information. These tools may use cookies or similar technologies to collect information about how visitors use our website.

5. Marketing Cookies

Where applicable, we may use advertising and marketing technologies to measure campaigns, understand conversions, and deliver more relevant advertising. These technologies may include services provided by platforms such as Meta or Google.

6. Managing Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control or delete cookies through their settings. You can choose to block or remove cookies, although doing so may affect certain website features or functionality.

7. Third-Party Services

Some services used on our website may be operated by third parties. These providers may place or access cookies according to their own privacy and cookie policies.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our website, services, technologies, or legal requirements. Any updates will be published on this page.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Cookie Policy or our use of cookies, please contact Webflora through the contact information provided on our website.
